⚙️ What Sidely Does

Sidely integrates ChatGPT seamlessly into Chrome.
You can chat, brainstorm, summarize text, translate, explain code, or generate ideas - all without leaving the page you’re on.

Unlike other extensions, Sidely connects directly to your own ChatGPT session - no extra logins, no third-party servers, and no risk to your privacy.

🔑 Key Features

  • One-click access to ChatGPT from any tab
  • Works with your existing OpenAI account - no registration or external logins
  • Lightweight and fast - minimal resource usage, instant load
  • Secure and private - no chat data or credentials ever leave your browser
  • Supports GPT-5 and GPT-4, depending on your OpenAI plan
  • Multi-purpose use: summarize articles, write emails, translate, debug, brainstorm, and more

💡 Why Choose Sidely

If you use ChatGPT daily - for work, study, or personal tasks - Sidely keeps it always accessible and distraction-free.
Stop wasting time switching between tabs. Sidely makes ChatGPT feel like a built-in part of Chrome.

Perfect for:

  • Copywriters and marketers
  • Developers and students
  • Researchers and content creators
  • Anyone who wants a faster workflow

🔒 Privacy-First Design

Your ChatGPT session runs locally in the Chrome sidebar via an embedded interface.
Sidely does not:

  • collect or store your chat data
  • access your OpenAI credentials
  • send any usage information outside your browser

You stay logged in to ChatGPT exactly as you would on chat.openai.com - but in a compact, convenient sidebar.
