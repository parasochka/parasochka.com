🤖 How Greekly works

  • Every exercise is generated by AI for your profile - name, city, hobbies and goals - and for the words you recently got wrong
  • An explanation after every answer - each mistake becomes a mini-lesson
  • Adaptive repetition - weak topics come back more often, mastered topics less often
  • Quiz - 20 questions per session on grammar, vocabulary and word order
  • Dialogue - an everyday conversation, from the taverna to the pharmacy, one line at a time
  • Letter - write a short message in Greek and get it corrected
  • Words - A2 vocabulary with repetition-first review and audio pronunciation
  • Sentence constructor - build Greek sentences from tiles and learn the word order
  • Statistics, streak and reminders - progress per topic and per word, a weekly overview, optional daily reminders
  • Pick up where you left off - unfinished exercises are saved

📚 Program and level

Greekly is a practice tool for Modern Greek from zero to A2: the language spoken in Greece and Cyprus today, not Ancient, Koine or Biblical Greek. Topics follow a structured A1 to A2 ladder: articles, cases, verb aspect, εσύ and εσείς, and the other things that trip up beginners, including the topics of the official A2 Greek language exam. It is a practice tool, not a teacher and not a certification.

The more you practise, the better Greekly understands what you need to work on next: AI uses your profile and your recent mistakes to generate the next exercise.

💳 Free plan and subscription

  • Free plan: 5 exercises a day for your first 6 days. No card, no email, no account.
  • Greekly Monthly: unlimited practice in all five modes, with a free first week for new subscribers. Price, length and renewal terms are shown before you buy, on the subscription screen.
  • On iPhone the subscription is bought through the App Store, renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and is managed in Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.
  • In Telegram the subscription is paid via Stripe and cancelled from the bot or by contacting support.

🎯 Who Greekly is for

  • Expats and digital nomads living in Greece or Cyprus
  • Learners preparing for the A2 Greek language exam
  • Anyone who already started Greek and wants daily, personalized practice
  • People who prefer short, focused sessions over long classes

🔒 Privacy

No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no third-party trackers. On iPhone Greekly never receives your email address. Payments go through Apple or Stripe and never reach Greekly's servers. You can delete your account, and everything attached to it, from inside the app. Details in the Privacy Policy.

⚠️ A note about AI

Most exercises are generated by artificial intelligence. Despite review, occasional inaccuracies are possible. If you spot one, report it from the “Report a problem” screen inside the app or the bot - it helps Greekly improve quickly.
