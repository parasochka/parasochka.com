🤖 How Greekly works

  • Every exercise is generated by AI for your profile - name, city, hobbies and goals - and for the words you recently got wrong
  • An explanation after every answer - each mistake becomes a mini-lesson
  • Adaptive repetition - weak topics come back more often, mastered topics less often
  • Quizzes - quick translation and grammar tasks
  • Dialogues - everyday Greek in realistic situations
  • Writing practice - short messages and emails
  • Vocabulary and sentence building - hand-written word and sentence banks
  • Listen button - hear any Greek phrase read aloud
  • Streak and reminders - a short session every day, on your schedule

📚 Program and level

Greekly is a practice tool for roughly A1-A2 level Modern Greek: grammar, vocabulary, dialogues and everyday writing, including the topics of the official A2 Greek language exam. It is a practice tool, not a teacher and not a certification.

The more you practise, the better Greekly understands what you need to work on next: AI uses your profile and your recent mistakes to generate the next exercise.

💳 Free plan and subscription

  • Free plan: a limited number of exercises per day for a limited number of days. No card, no email.
  • Subscription: unlimited practice. Price, length and renewal terms are shown before you buy, on the subscription screen.
  • On iPhone the subscription is bought through the App Store, renews automatically and is managed in Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.
  • In Telegram the subscription is paid via Stripe and cancelled from the bot or by contacting support.

🎯 Who Greekly is for

  • Expats and digital nomads living in Greece or Cyprus
  • Learners preparing for the A2 Greek language exam
  • Anyone who already started Greek and wants daily, personalized practice
  • People who prefer short, focused sessions over long classes

🔒 Privacy

No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no third-party trackers. On iPhone Greekly never receives your email address. Payments go through Apple or Stripe and never reach Greekly's servers. You can delete your account, and everything attached to it, from inside the app. Details in the Privacy Policy.

⚠️ A note about AI

Most exercises are generated by artificial intelligence. Despite review, occasional inaccuracies are possible. If you spot one, report it from the “Report a problem” screen inside the app or the bot - it helps Greekly improve quickly.
